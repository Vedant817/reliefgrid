# ReliefGrid three-minute demo

## 0:00-0:20 — The decision

Open `/?demo=1`. State one sentence: “A flood shelter needs 100 certified water filters in four hours; the suppliers still work through email.” Reset the controlled scenario and show the complete 100/100 plan.

## 0:20-0:50 — Email becomes evidence

Show one real AgentMail RFQ thread and supplier reply. In ReliefGrid, point to quantity, price, arrival, certification claim, and exact evidence spans. Show that an ambiguous field is blocked rather than guessed.

## 0:50-1:15 — Code decides

Open the allocation. The cheaper late offer loses; deterministic code selects the complete on-time mix. Emphasize that the LLM extracts language but cannot approve eligibility or spend.

## 1:15-2:05 — Hero moment: evidence drift

Open the public bulletin in a second tab. Run “Recheck changed source.” Firecrawl reads the public Convex route and confirms the NF-53 recall. Return to ReliefGrid: the selected offer is invalid, the old plan is superseded, and coverage falls from 100 to 30 without refresh.

## 2:05-2:35 — Human-controlled recovery

Show the cited causal change. Approve the in-thread hold notice, add controlled replacement stock, and show coverage return to 100. Approve the replacement plan; explain that synthetic suppliers are never emailed.

## 2:35-3:00 — Proof

Open the redacted decision packet and concise causal timeline. Finish on the Convex dashboard showing functions, data, workflow state, and live integrations. Do not open the chatbot, vector search, provider pagination, or full fixture history.

## Recording rules

- Keep the final video under three minutes.
- Show provider evidence only after a successful response.
- Label controlled data as synthetic.
- Do not display inbox addresses, API keys, raw tokens, or private email bodies.
- Record one uninterrupted successful run after rotating public-deployment credentials.
